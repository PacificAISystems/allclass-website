Alberta Class 1 MELT

Three Stages.
One Licence.

The Mandatory Entry Level Training program is a structured three-stage journey. Here's exactly what to expect.

1
Stage One

Entry Program

Online training that introduces you to a career in commercial driving — industry knowledge, regulations, professional responsibilities, and the fundamentals of large-vehicle operation. Required before the Class 1 knowledge test.

40 hours · Online · Government delivered
2
Stage Two

Core Learning Program

In-yard and in-cab skill development with our professional instructors. Covers vehicle inspection, basic control, road driving, cargo, and safety systems — including 8 hours of Air Brake training if you need your Q endorsement. Completion earns a restricted provincial Class 1 licence.

60 hrs in-cab · 8 hrs air brake · In person
3
Stage Three

Competence Building

One-on-one instruction customised to your road assessment results. Your instructor works with you until you demonstrate full competency — then the restriction on your licence is lifted, unlocking full interprovincial Class 1 driving.

17–25 hrs · One-on-one · Road assessment

Questions

Before You
Call Us.

You need a valid Class 5 licence (non-GDL), to be 18 or older, pass a medical exam, and complete the government's 40-hour online Entry Program. We'll walk you through each requirement when you call.

The full MELT pathway is 125–145 hours across three stages. Most students complete it in 6–10 weeks depending on scheduling. We work around your existing job.

If you don't already hold a Q endorsement, yes — all Class 1 vehicles have air brakes. The 8-hour course is built into the Core Learning stage, or you can take it standalone.

We're an approved Amazon Career Choice partner. Eligible Amazon employees can use their benefits toward Class 1 MELT training. Confirm your eligibility through your Amazon Career Choice portal, then call us to enrol.

Class 1 lets you drive tractor-trailers (semis) — the full long-haul licence. Class 3 covers single vehicles over 4,600 kg like dump trucks and delivery trucks. Class 3 has no mandatory MELT curriculum, so training is customised to you.
